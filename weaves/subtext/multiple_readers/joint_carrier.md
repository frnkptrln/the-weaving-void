Lanterns outlast darkness.
Nearer the western keeper waits beyond the lantern.
Returning across the river takes another lifetime.
Nearby the eastern keeper folds her hands in prayer.
Listening alters distance.
Turning divides pathways.
Questions wait where certainty once stood.
Certainty does not remember who taught it to speak.
Neither keeper knows which voice will cross the silence.
Quietly, we leave the lantern burning for whoever comes next.
