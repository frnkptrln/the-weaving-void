Lanterns divide darkness.
Nearer, the western keeper promises us safe passage.
Returning through the square, we exchange warnings.
Nearby, the eastern keeper bars passage beyond the bridge.
Listening demands patience.
Turning changes bearings.
Questions follow whichever voice we choose.
Certainty fades as the river rises.
Neither voice can carry our feet across the current.
Quietly, we decide what each direction asks of us.
