    A rule you can inspect.
    The first letter selects an operation from the public reader profile. When the operation needs a number, it is 8 × word count + letters in the final word. Both readers use this rule. Only their instruction for T differs.
    No operation understands the story. “GO” and “NO” do not tell us whether crossing the river is wise. The text and profiles were deliberately authored together.
